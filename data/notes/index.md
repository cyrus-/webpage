I'm an Associate Professor of Computer Science and Engineering at the
**University of Michigan**, where I lead the
[Future of Programming Lab (FP Lab)](http://fplab.mplse.org/), part of the
[MPLSE](http://mplse.org/) research community. We design **intelligent user
interfaces** for **modern programming languages**, ranging from type-theoretic
foundations through to human-centered design.

🆕 I am on sabbatical at the **University of Cambridge** for 2026–27, as a
Visiting Fellow in the [Energy and Environment Group](https://www.cst.cam.ac.uk/research/eeg)
in the [Department of Computer Science and Technology](https://www.cst.cam.ac.uk/)
and an Associate Scholar at [Pembroke College](https://www.pem.cam.ac.uk/).

**Email:** [comar@umich.edu](mailto:comar@umich.edu) · **Office:** 4624 BBB ·

## Projects

Most of our work lives in [Hazel](:hazel), a live functional programming
environment that can run incomplete programs, along with [Tylr](:tylr),
[Grove](:grove), [ChatLSP](:chatlsp), [Livelits](:livelits),
[Hazel Tutor](:hazel-tutor), [DeckFlow](:deckflow), [RustViz](:rustviz) and
[Bastion](:bastion). See [all projects](/projects) and [all papers](/papers).
