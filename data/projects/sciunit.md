title: "SciUnit: Collaborative Infrastructure for Test-Driven Scientific Model Validation"
date: "2012"
finish: "2014"
tags: [sciunit, science]
---

One of the pillars of the scientific method is model validation: comparing a
scientific model's predictions against empirical data. The
[SciUnit](https://github.com/scidash/sciunit) project casts this as a form of
collaborative software testing and develops useful infrastructure.
