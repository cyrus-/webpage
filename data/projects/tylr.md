---
title: "Tylr: Syntactic Completions with Material Obligations"
date: "2021"
tags: [tylr, structure-editing]
---

Parse errors are a pain for beginners and experts alike. The
[Tylr](https://tylr.fun) project eliminates parse errors (!) by automatically
tracking syntactic obligations and materializing them as holes and other
affordances. This benefits both humans and downstream tooling.
