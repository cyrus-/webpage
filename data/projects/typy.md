title: "typy: Programmable Semantic Fragments"
date: "2014"
finish: "2016"
tags: [typy, python]
---

[typy](https://github.com/cyrus-/typy) allows library providers to install new
statically typed semantic fragments into Python, while leaving its syntax alone
(which cleverly sidesteps the "expression problem"). We have applied typy to
problems in web security and data science.
