title: "Hazel: Live Functional Programming with Typed Holes"
date: "2016"
tags: [hazel, live-programming, typed-holes]
---

[Hazel](https://hazel.org) is a new kind of live programming environment that
can reason about and run incomplete programs, i.e. programs with holes of
various forms. Our publications develop the semantic foundations for live
hole-driven development.
