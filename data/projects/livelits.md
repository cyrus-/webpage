date: "2019"
finish: "2021"
tags: [livelits, hazel]
---

We are integrating graphical and programmatic modes of interaction by developing
language mechanisms that allow you to define type-specific user interfaces that
generate code underneath, i.e. they serve as interactive graphical literal
notation. See the [livelits demo](https://hazel.org/livelits).
