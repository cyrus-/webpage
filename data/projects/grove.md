---
title: "Grove: New Foundations for Collaborative Editing"
date: "2022"
tags: [grove, collaboration, structure-editing]
---

We are developing a new semantics for collaborative editing that dispenses with
diff-and-merge in favor of a commutative structure edit language. This resolves
a number of problems, particularly related to relocation conflicts, and opens up
new design avenues for a computational commons.
