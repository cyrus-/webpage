title: "Relit: Reasonably Programmable Literal Notation"
date: "2013"
finish: "2018"
tags: [relit, syntax, thesis]
---

My thesis research developed mechanisms that allow library providers to express
new type-specific literal notation (e.g. SQL literals) while ensuring that
client programmers can still reason abstractly and compositionally about types
and binding. Code: [cyrus-/relit](https://github.com/cyrus-/relit/).
